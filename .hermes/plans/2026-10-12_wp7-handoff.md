## The three fixes that closed `traj10`

`traj10` had drifted to 527 x-mismatches / 553 vvel / 539 raw-slot mismatches.
All three causes were pinned against the ROM, not guessed:

1. **`$129F` running ledge-launch** (`crates/sml2-core/src/player_sim.rs`).
   The landing check's "no floor" arm is not a plain fall: when
   `sMarioOnGround != 0` and `(hvel - 72) >= $78` in *byte* arithmetic — i.e.
   hvel `$C0-$FF` (running right) **or** `$00-$47` (running left) — the engine
   writes `vvel = 28` (`home.asm` `$129F`, the `ld a,28 / ld [sVVelocityIndex]`
   after `sub 72 / cp $78`). Evidence: traj10 f3555 — console hvel `$E8` off the
   ledge at y≈436 with `vvel = $1B` (28 set, then the frame's own gravity `dec`),
   sim fell with `vvel = 0`. Killed the f3555-3587 cluster (y, vvel, camy,
   ground, frame, hvel, plus the x/spx tail at 3572-3573).
2. **`$2DC6` bounce → the `$1DFE` ceiling probe reads the *shifted* Y**
   (`crates/sml2-core/src/objects.rs`). `$2E23` does `$FFC0 -= y_delta + 6`
   *before* calling `$1DFE`, so the probe is taken from the already-shifted Y and
   a hit adds `b` back. The port probed from the un-shifted Y (the comment even
   said "undoes the Y shift"). Evidence: traj10 f4603 — sim y 447 vs console
   460, then 500+ frames of divergence behind it. Killed the f4603-5129 cluster
   (x, camx, spx, vvel, sdir, mf/standby/active, raw slots).
3. **`$FF97 & 7` dither cadence** (`crates/sml2-runtime/src/main.rs`, replay
   loop). The engine increments `$FF97` once per frame (`$0300`/`$2729` wait on
   the VBlank flag, then `inc`), so the `$181C` integration always reads
   "previous residue + 1"; the capture samples `$FF97` at the PyBoy frame
   boundary and slips an extra increment on traj10 f2986/2988/2989. Verified by
   pulling `MarioSpeedTableH` out of the ROM at `$19FF` and comparing it
   byte-for-byte with the port's `SPEED_H` (identical) and then replaying the
   recorded x-deltas against both models: the pure per-frame counter explains
   every integration delta in the 5130-frame trajectory, the recorded phase
   fails exactly at f2988 (`+2` predicted vs `+1` console) and f2989. The fix
   keeps the record and only substitutes the cadence value when the record
   skips a step (`f.phase == expect + 1`) — it changes 3 frames in `traj10` and
   0 frames in `traj5`-`traj9`/`death1`/`death2`, whose per-logic-frame sampling
   breaks the cadence and therefore still wins with the record.
   `--play` now ticks `sim.phase` per frame too.

Do **not** re-try the "use the previous record's phase" experiment — it was
tried and reverted earlier the same day (x mismatches 527 → 4103 from frame 1).

## Verified facts worth keeping

- `SPEED_H`/`SPEED_V`/`SPEED_H_WATER` in `player_sim.rs` are byte-identical to
  the ROM (`$19FF`, `$18AF`, `$1AFF`); the index formula `hvel + ($FF97 & 7)` is
  the ROM's (`$181C`).
- The recorded `phase` field is not always the integration value — it is a
  capture-side artifact on isolated frames only (3 in 5130). Treat the record as
  authoritative everywhere else.
- The recorded `x`-delta at traj10 f2649 is *not* an integration step
  (`T[hvel+phase]` cannot produce it for any phase) — it comes from another
  path, and both the sim and the console agree on the resulting x. Don't chase
  it as a table bug.
- `$129F`'s launch fires in both running directions (the `sub 72` wraps).
